AI Leadership

Human Agency in an Age of Generative Tools

I approach generative AI as both a practical design capability and a question of human agency.

My work asks how emerging systems can expand experimentation without displacing authorship, judgment, ethical responsibility, or the productive difficulty through which designers learn.

I created CU Boulder's first generative AI design course, Generative Design with AI, because learning the tools was only half the problem. I wanted students from architecture, environmental product design, landscape architecture, and related disciplines to ask what happens to taste, bias, labor, environmental cost, and intellectual property when software starts participating in the creative process, and which decisions we should keep for ourselves.

I bring the same questions to architecture and design firms, schools, and professional organizations directly, through workshops, implementation strategy, talks, and consulting conversations about bringing generative tools into real practice without losing what makes the work good.

For Firms, Schools, and Institutions
Workshops, Strategy, and Talks
I work with architecture and design practices that want clear, informed guidance on generative AI rather than hype. Workshop content and implementation strategy come out of studio pedagogy, public speaking, and hands-on experimentation with these tools in real design settings. The goal is not automation for its own sake. It's better thinking, stronger communication, and a more resilient creative practice.
AI Workshops for Design Teams
Half-day and custom-format sessions for architecture and design offices, introducing practical AI workflows, prompting methods, and implementation opportunities grounded in real project work.
Implementation Strategy
Full-day, firm-facing sessions on where AI fits, where it doesn't, and how to adopt it with intention across representation, ideation, process, communication, and internal culture.
Talks and Lectures
Speaking engagements on AI, design pedagogy, creativity, authorship, and ethics for firms, schools, conferences, and professional audiences.
Consulting and Workflow Advising
Ongoing or one-time conversations for leaders who want help framing intentional, ethical AI use in their studio without losing authorship, judgment, or identity.
