AI in Design Education
Literacy Before Automation
I believe AI literacy is becoming part of design literacy, but its place in the curriculum should be deliberate, especially in foundational courses. Students need time to observe closely, draw, work with materials, tolerate uncertainty, and develop their own judgment. When an automated tool arrives before they understand the value of those experiences, efficiency can become a shortcut around learning.
Our students will help decide how these technologies enter the world and whose interests they serve. I want them fluent enough to use AI and critical enough to recognize when an exercise, project, or practice is stronger without it. That requires practical knowledge alongside serious attention to authorship, labor, bias, intellectual property, environmental cost, and the human capacities we choose not to delegate.
