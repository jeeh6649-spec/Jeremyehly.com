01 | Design Proposition

Make the ground floor count.

A useful street edge.
A quieter place above it.

The concrete base gives the project a clear address. Independent storefronts and a separate residential entrance support different rhythms of use, while the glazed upper floors and balconies give each home an outdoor connection.

The L-shaped residential plan leaves a shared outdoor room over the podium. Planting, shaded seating and a pool make that space a resident amenity. The lower retail roof carries planting rather than shared circulation.

01

Leasing Flexibility

Five storefront bays create a flexible commercial address. A café anchors the street experience, while complementary retail extends activity across the frontage.

02

Resident Experience

Private balconies, a shared pool and a planted garden give the residential offer a clear identity. The amenity program is designed around everyday use and long-term resident appeal.

03

Operating Discipline

Separate entries, dedicated service space, bicycle storage and two stair cores bring order to the building. Resident arrival and commercial activity each have a clear place.

04

Architectural Identity

A disciplined glass façade and projecting balconies rise above a grounded concrete base. The consistent architectural language gives the development a recognizable presence.

02 | Two Plans, One Design Direction

Put the proposal to work.

Street life at Level 1.
Shared life at Level 2.

The ground floor brings retail, residential arrival and parking into one organized plan. Above it, the L-shaped residential layout frames a garden terrace with a pool, outdoor seating and direct access from the common corridor.

Retail-led ground floor. Five storefront bays, a dedicated residential lobby, mail and parcels, two stair cores, an elevator, bicycle storage and service rooms.

15 parking spaces. The garage sits behind the active street frontage, with vehicle access and building services grouped along the side and rear.

11 homes on Level 2. Five one-bedroom homes, four two-bedroom homes and two studios form a varied residential mix, with private balconies and furnished living spaces.

A shared terrace reached from the common corridor. A dedicated passage connects the residences to a 15 × 28-foot pool, garden seating and planted outdoor spaces.

One Building.
Three Experiences.

The street: retail and a dedicated residential entrance create a continuous, active frontage.

The home: a diverse apartment mix pairs efficient living spaces with private outdoor connections.

The garden: a shared pool terrace extends residential life outdoors, above the activity of the street.
