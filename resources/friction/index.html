  4. Choosing to Stay Human

    Ethan Mollick | One Useful Thing | May 26, 2026

    Sorts what we hand to AI into settled, contested and unknown. Separates performance from learning, and shows how defaults decide what gets offloaded. The studies behind it are the Turkey math study, the Taipei Python study and the BCG consultants.

    In the talk | the three kinds of offloading, performance versus learning, and defaults

    oneusefulthing.org

  5. RIBA Artificial Intelligence Report 2026

    Royal Institute of British Architects | August 2026

    The third RIBA survey of AI in practice, fielded March to April 2026 with more than 1,100 respondents. Adoption rose from 41% to 59% to 74% across the 2024, 2025 and 2026 surveys. Respondents chose to take part, so read the figures as a signal and not a census.

    In the talk | adoption over time, productivity versus better designs, and worry about early-career skills

    Report pageFull PDF

  6. Basgier and Wardle on values guiding AI use

    Basgier and Wardle | Substack | April 15, 2026

    A framework for letting values guide when and how we use AI. It starts from what we care about, then takes one step at a time. It was written for teaching, and it works for any practice that has to decide what to hand over and what to keep.

    In the talk | values first, one step

    christopherbasgier.substack.com
