# Chengdu Living Bridge

**Role:** Design, Drawings, 3D Modeling, Rendering
**Firm:** MVRDV
**Collaborators:** MVRDV, two-person project team (other member not identified in the current source)
**Location:** Chengdu, China
**Year:** 2008
**Status:** Second-place competition proposal
**Tags:** Landscape Architecture | Architecture | Speculative

An inhabitable ecological corridor spanning the river at the center of MVRDV's Chengdu water city masterplan, combining transportation, playground, public space, and landscape in a single structure. A triangulated concrete truss carries vehicular traffic below while a continuous planted surface rises from grade on either bank to form a park across the water, folding a highway crossing into a piece of civic landscape rather than treating it as pure infrastructure.
