Bologna Train Station

Citta Sospesa|MVRDV|Bologna, Italy|2007 to 2008
An inhabitable piece of infrastructure, built as a new public level above the rail tracks.

Citta Sospesa, Italian for Suspended City, proposes a new elevated level built above Bologna Central Station's rail tracks, continuing the city's urban fabric and reconnecting its northern and southern districts. Streets, plazas, and public space sit atop the station in a contemporary interpretation of local arcades, with a mixed-use program of hotel, office, residential, and masterplan components across 200,000 square meters.

Openings cut through the elevated square to create visual connections between the public deck and the platforms below, letting daylight reach the trains and letting travelers glimpse the city above. Ramps, elevators, and escalators keep the new level accessible, while rooftop areas offer relaxation space with views across Bologna.

Project Facts
Invited international competition entry for RFI (Rete Ferroviaria Italiana), budget 340 million Euro. 200,000 square meters of hotel, office, residential, and masterplan program.
