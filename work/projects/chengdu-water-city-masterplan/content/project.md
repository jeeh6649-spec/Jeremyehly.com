# Chengdu Water City Masterplan

**Role:** Design, 3D Modeling, Layout
**Firm:** MVRDV
**Location:** Chengdu, China
**Year:** 2008
**Status:** Under construction
**Tags:** Architecture | Built Work

A city-scale masterplan organizing a cluster of islands within an expanded river channel into a mixed-use water city, connected back to the existing urban grid by the Chengdu Living Bridge. Seven urban identities, from a high-density mixed downtown to campus, park, city block, and low-rise sub-urban housing, each carry their own rules for density, height, and architectural character, giving a single planning framework room for genuine variety block to block. A phased development strategy sequences the islands and waterfront across multiple stages of construction.
