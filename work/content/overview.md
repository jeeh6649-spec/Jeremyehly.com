# Work

Selected professional, speculative, built, and formative academic work across architecture, landscape architecture, product design, and visual communication.

Across the architecture firms I have had the pleasure of being part of, I have always felt most at home generating ideas, then pouring myself into the digital models and renderings that make those spaces visible. On occasion, I have also had the extraordinary pleasure of following the work through and helping bring it into the world. And yes, I did plenty of Revit too, if you're asking.
