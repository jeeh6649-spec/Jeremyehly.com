Jeremy Ehly

Licensed Architect|Design Educator|Ethical AI in Design Practice|CU Boulder
Designer, educator, and advocate for thoughtful human agency in an age of rapidly changing tools.

Everything you see on this site grew out of collaboration: with students discovering what they are passionate about and helping to nurture that urge, with colleagues and fellow designers getting excited about what we are putting out into the world, and more recently with generative AI as a fascinating and capable (if not troubled) new creative partner. The thing that stays constant across all the work is a shared appetite for design that is creatively ambitious, communicates beautifully and viscerally, and takes responsibility for what it adds to our world.

What could we design together?
