Jeremy Ehly

Licensed Architect|Design Educator|Ethical AI in Design Practice|CU Boulder
Designer, educator, and advocate for thoughtful human agency in an age of rapidly changing tools.

Everything on this site grew out of collaboration: with students working out what they believe in, with colleagues and fellow architects arguing over one more detail, and lately with generative AI as a strange and occasionally brilliant new creative partner. The thing that stays constant across all of it is a shared appetite for work that is genuinely ambitious, communicates viscerally, and takes responsibility for what it puts into the world.
