# Teaching

Powerful design education unites rigorous thinking with meaningful doing.

The students drawn to Environmental Design at CU Boulder care deeply about the world they are inheriting and want to help change it for the better. Jeremy teaches to help them give that concern purposeful form through the products, buildings, landscapes, and communities they design and make.

Across more than fifteen years of teaching at the University of Colorado Boulder, he has developed learning environments in which students investigate why a project matters, learn through iteration and failure, build confidence in their own judgment, and carry ideas into physical and public consequence.

His teaching spans foundational studios, environmental product design, architecture and product capstones, design-build, visual communication, theory and ethics, ecological design, digital modeling, and generative AI. Across these settings, technology is taught in relation to human experience, ecological responsibility, authorship, and care.

The selected courses are browsable through the same public categories used in the curriculum vitae: Studios, Design-Build, Seminars, and Emerging Technology + Digital Practice. Foundation, advanced, and capstone levels remain visible on individual course cards. AI Collaboration may appear as an additional marker where relevant.
