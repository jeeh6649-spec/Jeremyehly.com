# Teaching

Powerful design education unites rigorous thinking with meaningful doing.

Across more than fifteen years of teaching at the University of Colorado Boulder, Jeremy Ehly has developed learning environments in which students investigate why a project matters, learn through iteration and failure, build confidence in their own judgment, and carry ideas into physical and public consequence.

His teaching spans foundational studios, environmental product design, architecture and product capstones, design-build, visual communication, theory and ethics, ecological design, digital modeling, and generative AI. Across these settings, technology is taught in relation to human experience, ecological responsibility, authorship, and care.

The selected courses should be browsable by studio level or Seminar / Special Topics. AI Collaboration may appear as an additional marker where relevant. Teaching-philosophy pillars remain internal descriptors used for editorial consistency and related-content recommendations.
