# Mosquito Management Ecosystem

Pest management becomes an ecological design problem rather than a chemical response.

The proposal uses drainage, rain gardens, riparian planting, shade, and habitat infrastructure to reduce mosquito breeding conditions while supporting the species that prey on them. A bat pavilion and snake park are situated within a broader water and vegetation strategy, connecting product design to site performance.

The project communicates implementation as carefully as intent. Species behavior, construction, placement, maintenance, and safety are translated into product guides, prototypes, and a detailed physical site model.
