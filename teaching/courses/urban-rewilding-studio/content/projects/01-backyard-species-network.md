# Backyard Species Network

A residential landscape becomes a negotiated habitat for native plants, birds, snakes, pollinators, and a domestic cat.

The proposal links planting and product interventions rather than treating them as separate additions. Seasonal food sources, sheltered movement, nesting opportunities, predator relationships, and household routines establish a layered ecological network.

Species-specific products make those relationships tangible. A cat oasis channels play and hunting behavior within a controlled space. Mycelium birdhouses support nesting while introducing a biodegradable material cycle. An underground bullsnake refuge uses familiar landscape construction to make coexistence with an often-misunderstood species more legible.
