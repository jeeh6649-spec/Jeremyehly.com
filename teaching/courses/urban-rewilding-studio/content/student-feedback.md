## Evidence for Revision

Student feedback also identified the cost of fitting a new interdisciplinary curriculum into eight weeks. Students asked for more time on the final proposal, fewer early checkpoints, clearer grading criteria, and a more even balance between ecological research and design development. These comments directly inform the provisional reflection and the next course iteration.
