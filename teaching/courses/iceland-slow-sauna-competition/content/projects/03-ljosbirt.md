# Ljósbirt | Sauna and Greenhouse

Descent, immersion, emergence.

Ljósbirt grows from the volcanic earth of Mývatn as a buried corridor beneath turf and stone. From this passage, two masses extend outward: a sauna that barely emerges from the ground and a greenhouse released toward the sky.

Each space holds a different temperature, quality of light, and stage of ritual. The corridor compresses into darkness, the sauna turns the body inward, and the greenhouse opens the gaze toward the crater and sky. Architecture is understood as a continuous sequence in which each transition must be earned.
