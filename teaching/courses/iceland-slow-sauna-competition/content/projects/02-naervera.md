# Nærvera

Presence through seasonal ritual and shared heat.

Nærvera extends the principles of slow travel into an architecture stripped to essential experiences. Like a traditional Icelandic turf house, the structure gathers people around a single hearth, reconnecting sauna and sunroom through warmth, light, and gathering.

The proposal responds to a world in which information, sound, and light can be turned on in the blink of an eye. Here, there is no electric light, even in winter. Summer supports cultivation, long views, and abundant daylight. Winter relies on fire and candlelight, asking visitors to accept darkness as part of the ritual. The architecture becomes a framework for disconnecting from modern habits, being present, and embracing the season.
