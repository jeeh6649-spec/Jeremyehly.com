# Lækna

An architecture shaped by the forces of the land.

Lækna draws from Iceland's basalt lava tubes: fluid, vaulted spaces formed as flowing lava hardens at the surface while remaining molten within. The proposal translates that geological logic into an architecture that appears to grow from the site.

Curving forms, natural materials, and a closely integrated plan connect sauna, greenhouse, shower, and gathering spaces to the surrounding terrain. The project frames climate, sensory experience, and ritual as parts of a single restorative environment for health, growth, and relaxation.
