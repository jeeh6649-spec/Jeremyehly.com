# Jumble

Jumble is a modular indoor planting system built from truncated-octahedron pods connected through an alternating magnetic pattern. Users can assemble the units intuitively into playful, expandable arrangements rather than treating plants as isolated pots.

The design developed through failed structures, greenhouse testing, growing-medium experiments, and repeated prototypes. Its final form joins digitally produced components with living material while keeping the system legible and approachable.
