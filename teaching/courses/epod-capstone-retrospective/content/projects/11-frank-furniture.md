# Frank Furniture

Frank Furniture responds to the waste generated by short-term living and frequently discarded furniture. Two CNC-cut modular components join without external fasteners or tools to form shelves, desks, chairs, tables, benches, and larger assemblies.

The system can be disassembled, reconfigured, transported, repaired, shared, or expanded by combining pieces from multiple users. Its formal simplicity supports a longer material life and an alternative to disposable furnishings.
