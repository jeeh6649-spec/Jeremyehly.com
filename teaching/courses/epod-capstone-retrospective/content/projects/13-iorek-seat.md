# IOREK Seat

IOREK is a modular seating system designed for young adults who move frequently and may rely on small vehicles. The system disassembles in minutes, nests into a compact box, and can be transported by one person.

Interlocking panels, removable cushions, simple fasteners, and configurable arrangements balance mobility with the comfort and presence of larger furniture. Customizable colors and layouts allow the system to adapt to new homes rather than behave like anonymous temporary furniture.
