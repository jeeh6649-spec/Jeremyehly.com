# Rescue Light

Rescue Light is a lightweight, collapsible winter rescue sled designed for faster response and easier transport across advanced and backcountry terrain. An inflatable sled supports patient comfort and packs into a wearable backpack when not deployed.

The design reduces the need for two-person handling and accepts a standard stretcher insert for transfer between emergency-care settings. Research, mentor feedback, material studies, digital templates, and sewn fabrication informed a functional half-scale prototype.
