# Dragonfly Table

The Dragonfly dining set translates a family adoption story into contemporary furniture. Dragonfly inlays reference a moment associated with good fortune, happiness, and love, while the table and chairs combine precedents from Ming-dynasty furniture with a contemporary Chinese-American identity.

The project developed through client conversations, historical research, concept refinement, digital and physical modeling, and full-scale woodworking. Solid cherry and traditional joinery give the pieces durability intended to support use across generations.
