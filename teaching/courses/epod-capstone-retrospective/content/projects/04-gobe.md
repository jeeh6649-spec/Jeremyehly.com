# Gobe

Gobe is a line of moisture-controlled bags designed to protect electronics in humid environments where climate-controlled storage is uncommon. Rechargeable silica packs absorb moisture, change color when saturated, and can be restored through accessible heat sources.

The project connects product protection to digital access. Its first application prioritizes affordability, durability, cross-language usability, and the conditions faced by users for whom the failure of a laptop may mean losing access to education or the internet.
