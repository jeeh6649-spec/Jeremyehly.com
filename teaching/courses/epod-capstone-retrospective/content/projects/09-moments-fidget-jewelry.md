# Moments | Adult Fidget Jewelry

Moments is a line of sterling-silver fidget jewelry created for adults with ADHD, dyslexia, and related attention needs. Rings, necklaces, bracelets, and earrings provide discreet tactile movement while remaining appropriate for professional and social settings.

The project reframes an assistive behavior as something elegant and personally meaningful. Function is integrated into familiar forms of adornment rather than expressed as separate medical equipment.
