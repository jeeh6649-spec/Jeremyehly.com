# Vegi

Vegi is a modular indoor garden designed to make growing edible plants more accessible in apartments and other spaces without adequate daylight. A self-watering planter supports approximately ten days of autonomy, while an integrated timed grow light allows the unit to operate away from a window.

The wooden frame can be stacked or extended with open modules that also function as shelving. The full-scale prototype combined 3D-printed planter components, CNC-milled plywood, integrated electrical routing, and custom LED assembly.
