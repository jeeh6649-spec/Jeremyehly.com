# Hidden Gem Tea

Hidden Gem Tea reimagines tea packaging as an unfolding experience of discovery. Color, geometry, nested components, and a small metallic tea container turn opening the package into a sequence intended to lift mood and create surprise.

The final system emerged through repeated physical prototypes, material studies, laser-cut revisions, opening tests, identity development, and packaging templates. The project demonstrates how brand, object, and interaction can operate as one designed experience.
