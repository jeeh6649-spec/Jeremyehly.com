# The Entendre Chair

The Entendre Chair uses multifunctional furniture to open a conversation about intimacy, privacy, and sex-positive domestic life. Its mid-century-inspired form is designed to operate as a comfortable lounge chair while supporting more intimate uses without requiring a separate or concealed object.

The project treats playfulness and sexual expression as legitimate subjects of home design, challenging the assumption that intimacy must remain visually or spatially disconnected from everyday living.
