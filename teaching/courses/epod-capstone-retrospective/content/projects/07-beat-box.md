# BEAT BOX

BEAT BOX addresses the difficulty of storing and transporting a complete drum kit. It combines the footprint of a full set, the protection of a road case, and the compactness of a travel kit so that one person can move the instrument in a single trip.

Digital modeling, a full-scale mock-up, hardware development, and mechanical refinement established proof of concept. The resulting prototype fits in a car trunk while retaining the sound, physical spacing, and playability expected from a conventional drum set.
