# Critical Reflections

After a semester of working intensively with generative tools, students completed one assignment without AI-assisted drafting or editing. They were asked to state an honest personal position on authorship, creative agency, professional change, environmental cost, and the value of making.

> "It will be crucial to stay in the driver seat of your train of thought."

> "The water consumption it took to create my video, honestly, made me feel greedy."

> "Without the process of creating and thinking for myself, the meaning of the work feels hollow."

> "But could a life with AI be too efficient?"

*Anonymous student reflections, Spring 2026. Excerpts represent differing individual positions rather than a single course viewpoint.*
