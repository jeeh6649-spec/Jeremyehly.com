# EPOP Retail Outlet

**EPOD 3100 | Fall 2019 | Jeremy Ehly + Jesse Van Horne**

Design, make, sell.

EPOP Retail Outlet joined product design, design-build, entrepreneurship, branding, and public engagement in one collective studio. Students proposed and constructed a mobile retail environment, developed products for sale, established merchandising and operational systems, and opened the result to the public.

The project arrived at an important moment in the formation of Environmental Products of Design. The outlet became both a practical sales environment and an expression of the program's emerging identity. Students had to ask what responsible retail could mean within a culture of material abundance and environmental consequence, while also creating an experience capable of communicating the value of student design.

## An Immersive Studio

+ **Retail + Consumer Research** | Students examined customer experience, precedent, circulation, display, transport, branding, and contemporary questions about consumption.
+ **Collaborative Proposals** | Teams developed distinct pop-up systems and communicated structure, configuration, cost, assembly, accessibility, and merchandising.
+ **Design + Build** | The class selected and developed a shared direction through budgeting, scheduling, digital fabrication, analog construction, and installation.
+ **Products + Market** | Each student designed objects of consequence, moving from concept through fabrication, branding, display, and public sale.

The final outcome connected decisions that are often separated in school. Product, architecture, identity, website, signage, packaging, merchandising, budget, fabrication, and operation all had to support the same public experience.
