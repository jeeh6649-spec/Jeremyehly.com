# Graphic Design and Portfolio

**ENVD 4052-001 | Spring 2025 | Sixteen-week workshop**

Professional identity as a design problem.

Graphic Design and Portfolio connected foundational visual communication to a practical outcome with lasting value: a coherent professional identity expressed through a PDF portfolio, resume, cover letter, website, and professional network.

The course began with typography, color, composition, hierarchy, image, and visual analysis. Short exercises and poster projects gave students a shared vocabulary for critique and helped them see graphic design as more than surface styling. Those principles were then applied to a longer process of editing, sequencing, clarifying, and presenting work developed across architecture, landscape architecture, planning, and environmental product design studios.

## Three Connected Phases

+ **Graphic Design Foundations** | Weekly exercises developed control of type, color, composition, visual hierarchy, and message.
+ **PDF Portfolio** | Students identified an audience, curated evidence, revised project narratives, created a visual system, and assembled a professional body of work.
+ **Web Portfolio + Professional Presence** | The portfolio system extended to a website, resume, cover letter, and professional network.

The workshop format made iteration visible. Students continuously presented work in progress, compared approaches, received peer and instructor critique, and revised both individual spreads and the larger sequence. The final review placed students in conversation with practicing architects, furniture designers, and landscape architects, closely approximating the experience of applying for professional work or graduate study.

## Responsible AI in Professional Communication

Generative AI was introduced as an editing and development tool, not a substitute for design intention. Students could use AI to clarify writing, test visual directions, or strengthen existing project material, but they were expected to explain how it was used and remain responsible for originality, accuracy, copyright, and the final quality of the work.
