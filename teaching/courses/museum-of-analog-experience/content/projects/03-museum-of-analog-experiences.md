# Museum of Analog Experiences

This proposal creates an open civic institution organized around a central atrium. Galleries, performance space, an escape room, food, making, and informal gathering are linked through visible circulation and shared daylight. The program invites visitors to participate rather than remain passive observers.

A screened facade mediates between interior activity and the Golden Triangle. It filters light, gives the building a recognizable public presence, and opens at street level to support curiosity and access. The animated walkthrough extends the portfolio sequence by showing how the atrium connects the museum's social and cultural programs.
