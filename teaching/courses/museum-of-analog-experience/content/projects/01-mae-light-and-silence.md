# MAE | Light and Silence

This proposal organizes the museum through shifts in light, scale, sound, compression, and openness. Narrow passages slow movement before releasing visitors into luminous galleries and landscaped courts. The sequence makes attention physical: orientation is established through the body as much as through signage or program.

The restrained material palette allows daylight, shadow, water, and occupation to become the primary sources of variation. The museum becomes a calibrated passage between the intensity of the city and quieter forms of shared presence.
