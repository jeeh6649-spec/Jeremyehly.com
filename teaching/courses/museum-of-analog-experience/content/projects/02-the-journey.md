# The Journey

The Journey treats circulation as the museum's central exhibit. Ramps, stairs, atriums, bridges, and exterior passages connect spaces for craft, fabrication, performance, gathering, and reflection. Movement through the building continually reframes both the creative work inside and the city beyond it.

An interactive copper facade extends that idea through time. Its surface is designed to change through weathering and use, allowing the building to register transformation rather than present itself as a fixed object. Analog making, material change, and social circulation form a single public experience.
