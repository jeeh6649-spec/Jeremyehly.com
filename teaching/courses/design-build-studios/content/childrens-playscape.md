# Children's Playscape

**ENVD 3300-030 | Spring 2017 | Northeast Denver Housing Center**

Students designed and constructed a play environment for an affordable housing community in West Denver. The project began with interviews and design workshops involving residents and children, grounding the work in the needs of the community rather than an assumed program.

The studio defined a playscape as an outdoor learning environment supporting social, emotional, cognitive, and physical development. Proposals were evaluated through four connected criteria: lasting play value, buildability, aesthetic quality, and safety.

## From Community Input to Construction

Students moved through research, precedent studies, site and program analysis, community outreach, concept design, prototyping, budgeting, scheduling, construction documentation, permitting, fabrication, and on-site assembly.

The completed environment combines climbing, sliding, swinging, balancing, and open-ended play within a shared landscape. The client intended the project and its community-centered process to serve as a prototype for future playgrounds within affordable housing developments across Denver.
