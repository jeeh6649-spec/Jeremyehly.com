# Children's Outdoor Classroom

**ENVD 3300-060 | Spring 2019 | Denver Botanic Gardens**

A semi-enclosed space at the Morrison Discovery Center became a flexible three-season environment for children's education, planting, cooking demonstrations, celebrations, and unscripted play.

The syllabus positioned the studio as an interdisciplinary design-build practice. Students interviewed the client, established the program, researched children and educational environments, developed the design through professional project phases, managed cost and scheduling, fabricated components, and completed construction within a sixteen-week semester.

The resulting classroom combines modular furniture, adjustable tables, storage, planting areas, seating, work surfaces, and illuminated hexagonal elements. The material and formal language connects the space to the broader identity and educational mission of Denver Botanic Gardens.

## Learning Through Delivery

The project required students to connect design intent to professional responsibility. Client presentations, construction documents, procurement, fabrication, risk management, and on-site coordination became parts of one continuous process.

The course archive records the result succinctly: **on time and on budget.** At the time of the course, the syllabus anticipated that the classroom would support approximately 12,000 visitors each year.
