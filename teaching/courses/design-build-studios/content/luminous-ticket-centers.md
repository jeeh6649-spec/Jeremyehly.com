# Luminous Ticket Centers

**ENVD 3300-050 + ENVD 3200-001 | Spring + Maymester 2018 | Denver Botanic Gardens**

Two mobile ticket centers replaced temporary winter tents with illuminated structures designed around staff comfort, visitor arrival, mobility, and the visual identity of Denver Botanic Gardens.

The course joined a sixteen-week design studio with a three-week Maymester build. Students began with a small birdhouse exercise focused on craft, materials, weather resistance, and precise detailing. They then interviewed staff and volunteers, studied operational needs, and developed dozens of proposals. The client selected a direction combining elements from several finalists.

The spring studio moved through concept design, schematic design, design development, and construction documentation. During Maymester, the class became an intensive fabrication and construction team. Small groups addressed structure, lighting, HVAC, interior layout, signage, waterproofing, mobility, cost, scheduling, and highway dimensional requirements. The completed units use illuminated acrylic facades and digitally fabricated interior elements to create compact public architecture that remains functional in winter conditions.

The project was subsequently recognized through an Architecture MasterPrize and a Denver Mayor's Design Award. These honors affirmed both the quality of the completed structures and the value of a teaching model in which students carry a public project from research and client engagement through fabrication and use.
