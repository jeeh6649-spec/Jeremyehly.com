Design Education

Studios & Seminars

I believe powerful design education unites rigorous thinking with the discipline of actually making things.

Across more than fifteen years of teaching at the University of Colorado Boulder, I've watched students learn why a project matters, work through iteration and failure, build confidence in their own judgment, and carry ideas into physical and public consequence.

My teaching spans foundational studios, environmental product design, architecture and product capstones, design-build, visual communication, theory and ethics, ecological design, digital modeling, and generative AI.
